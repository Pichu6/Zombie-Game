# 0018 · Answers on role-gated orders, world history, virus editor, cure and no-virus mode

- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** Decided by the user.
- **Answers:** questions 1–5 from [vision](../vision.md) after [0016](0016-sandbox-setup-and-layer-flow.md).

## Decisions

1. **Role-gated orders:** playing micro → macro, you can only give the orders your character's role allows. In the other play styles you can take any role.
2. **The world is complete at the start:** cities and pops already exist when the world is created; no history is simulated before play. During play, towns can grow or fall over game time, and a player can watch that happen (for example as a spectator).
3. **Virus editor uses sliders**, not points. Trait cards remain as named bundles of slider changes; there is no point budget.
4. **Cure is a sandbox option** with its own customisation settings (treatment window, vaccine feasibility, whether a turned body can be cured, research difficulty). Default (Proposal until the user says otherwise): treatment works before turning, a vaccine is a lab project, nothing cures a turned zombie.
5. **"No virus" is in the base game:** with no outbreak the game becomes city building, world management, political decisions and a Sims-like life.
