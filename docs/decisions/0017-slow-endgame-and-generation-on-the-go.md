# 0017 · Slow, hard endgame; infrastructure falls without upkeep; generation on the go

- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** Decided by the user (intent below). Models are Proposals in [items and crafting](../design/items-crafting.md#endgame-and-upkeep) and [world simulation](../design/world-simulation.md) section 12.
- **Extends:** [0013](0013-production-ladder.md), [0016](0016-sandbox-setup-and-layer-flow.md).

## Decisions

- **The late game in the Individual layer** is better quality items and resources, the need for machinery, collaboration and trade.
- **All of it can fail.** If no pop goes to inspect and repair the power station, there is no power, and it is worse when repairs depend on scavenged parts.
- **Slow and grindy, nothing easy, as in real life.** References: Haven & Hearth (with its charts, stats and info screens) and Space Engineers survival.
- **The power plant across layers:** ordered in Macro for a sector or zone, seen being built or working in City, and played from inside by a pop who has to repair or run it in Individual.
- **Generation:** terrain, building and interior (props) generation, on the go, reusing parts without feeling repetitive.

## Why

The user wants the endgame to be the real work of keeping a society running, where effort and organisation, not shortcuts, decide what survives.
