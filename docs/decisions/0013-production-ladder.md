# 0013 · Manufacturing is capped by machinery; salvage is easy, making is late game

- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Status:** Decided by the user (intent below). The model in [items and crafting](../design/items-crafting.md#production-ladder) is a Proposal.
- **Extends:** [0006](0006-crafting-quality.md) (quality formula still open) and [0012](0012-world-simulation-layers.md).

## Decisions

- Everything can still be dismantled and built, but making some things is capped by the machinery and infrastructure it needs: engines, car body parts, concrete, buildings, power and water network parts.
- Salvaging is much easier than manufacturing (scavenging a wall is easier than making the concrete).
- Manufacturing is late-game play that needs teamwork, groups and trade, as in Haven & Hearth.
- Some qualities are rarely reached, only when the whole production chain runs well.

## Why

The user wants the late game to be rebuilding production, not only looting, and wants top quality to reward a well-run settlement and its supply chain rather than one skilled crafter.
