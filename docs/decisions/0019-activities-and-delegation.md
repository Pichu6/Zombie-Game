# 0019 · Repetitive work can be delegated; many hands-on activities in the Individual layer

- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** Decided by the user (intent below). The model in [activities](../design/activities.md) is a Proposal.
- **Answers:** the open question from [0017](0017-slow-endgame-and-generation-on-the-go.md).

## Decisions

- **Repetitive work can always be handed to pops;** the player's own hands do the skilled or dangerous step. The work stays slow and hard, without tedious clicking.
- **Plenty of activities, like the mini-games in Shenmue:** lots of things to do, especially in the Individual layer, in both the isometric and over-the-shoulder cameras.
- **Every activity is something pops do too,** so the player can do whatever pops do.

## Why

The user wants every possessed life to have plenty to do by hand, while the simulation's rule that player and pops are equal still holds.
