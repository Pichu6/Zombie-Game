# 0007 · Human body

- **Original date:** recorded in the prototype (2026-09); brought into the new project on 2026-10-05
- **Status:** Decided by the user in the prototype. Revalidate if anything changed with our own engine.

## Decisions

- Real human anatomy is the main reference. Fix the skin; never shrink bones to make them fit.
- Filmed real people are the primary reference; other games are secondary.
- Realistic variety of bodies, faces, chests and glutes. The body changes in game with exercise and food.
- Strength and stamina come from the body; traits like "skinny" or "fit" only describe it.
- Muscles deform, grow and atrophy. Fat affects the face and double chin.
- Layers: an animatable skeleton, skin with morphs, and a light interior revealed by damage or decay. No per-vertex simulation.
- The skeleton is the rig's structure and the visible end of decomposition.
- Scapula and back move with the arm. Eyes belong to the organ layer.
- Male and female bases each get their own anatomy, under the same review criteria.
- All body variants share topology and identity.
- Characters are reviewed from every view, not only the front.
