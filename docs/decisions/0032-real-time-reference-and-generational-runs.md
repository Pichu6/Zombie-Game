# 0032 · Real time and real Earth data as the reference; 1 hour = 1 day as normal play; generational runs

- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** Decided by the user (intent below). Answers the default-speed question of [0031](0031-restoring-capabilities-real-time-playable-age.md) and refines the time scale of [vision](../vision.md) and [0021](0021-recommended-defaults.md).

## Decisions

1. **Real time and real Earth data are the default for everything:** every rate, duration and starting value is the real-life one unless a setting changes it.
2. **Playing at 1 real hour per game day, and speeding up, slowing down or pausing, are normal:** real time is the reference, not the speed you are expected to sit through.
3. **Generational runs are a use of the game:** with no outbreak, speed time up and watch a couple of generations of pops go by, to see whether they behave realistically.

## How it fits what was decided before

- Durations are real ([0031](0031-restoring-capabilities-real-time-playable-age.md)); only the clock speed changes. The 1 hour = 1 day speed is the usual choice, so the old "1× = 1 hour per game day" wording becomes "the usual speed", and 1:1 real time is a named speed.
- Bodies still walk at real speed ([0021](0021-recommended-defaults.md)).
- Realism of long runs is judged against real data, never by feel (see [sandbox parameters](../design/sandbox-parameters.md) §13.1).
