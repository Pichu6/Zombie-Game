# 0031 · Realistic numbers for worlds, and the loop between pops and nations

- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** Decided by the user (intent below, from their message in chat). The laws and numbers are Proposals until researched and confirmed: see [world numbers](../design/world-numbers.md) once written.
- **Extends:** [0016](0016-sandbox-setup-and-layer-flow.md) (generated worlds), [0028](0028-real-time-reference-and-generational-runs.md), [0030](0030-nation-demographics.md).

## Decisions

1. **Realistic numbers matter more than Earth 1:1.** Names, places and cultures need not copy Earth; the numbers must be realistic: how big a world is, what resources it has, how many people it can hold, how they are spread, how nations arise and how pops are divided among them.
2. **The loop is the point.** Pops shape what a nation can do (workers, soldiers, taxpayers, scholars), and the nation's capability in turn shapes the pops (security, services, growth), as upkeeping a city in Cities: Skylines or running an empire in Stellaris.
3. **Realistic data for a better simulation:** the numbers come from real sources with citations, never invented and presented as real.

## Why

The user wants the world, its nations and its people to behave like a real, believable system, so that a generated world is a laboratory and a game and not a set of random sliders.
