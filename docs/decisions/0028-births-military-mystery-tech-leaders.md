# 0028 · Births, military power, mystery outbreak, tech tree, leaders you can play

- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** Decided by the user (intent below, answering the six questions of [sandbox parameters](../design/sandbox-parameters.md)). Models in that doc are Proposals.
- **Extends:** [0027](0027-no-outbreak-game-and-parameter-catalogue.md). Refines "finite world, no respawn" ([0005](0005-finite-world-parity.md)): births are real events, not respawns.

## Decisions

1. **Births are part of the simulation**, with ageing and death, which gives the Stellaris kind of play: you put leaders and researchers in place, and they age and die over time.
2. **Military power counts in the simulation**, regardless of combat gameplay. Combat gameplay should exist in some form and at some later time.
3. **Late outbreak and mystery:** an outbreak may or may not happen, and the player may not know whether it is a normal epidemic or a zombie apocalypse. An **off option** must exist.
4. **A Stellaris-style technology tree is wanted.** Its shape is to be decided (tbd).
5. **Everything must make sense in the simulation** (one contract, conservation, causes). The hard part, and where the innovation lies, is levels of detail and pop levels across the layers.
6. **Officeholders are real pops, selectable in the higher layers:** a president the player likes in Layer 3 can be followed and played in Layer 1.

## Notes (not decisions)

- War between nations is therefore in scope as a simulated process; the earlier recommendation to leave it out is withdrawn.
- The earlier recommendation of "no tech tree" is withdrawn; how the tree fits the production ladder ([0013](0013-production-ladder.md)) is open.
- Open items stay listed in [sandbox parameters](../design/sandbox-parameters.md) §15.
