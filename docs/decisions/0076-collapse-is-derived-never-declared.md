# 0076 · Collapse is derived, never declared: society fails only through what pops do at their stations

- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Status:** Decided by the user. The user's words (lightly cleaned): "Sandbox options that require a job and station mechanics should have in mind the collapse of society. Reactions and decisions of pops should be the ones that create that, so the sim is honest: everything is a reaction of something, from the world simulation to pops and society, all tied up to one another." The mechanism below is a default taken by Claude ([0052](0052-one-executable-one-simulation.md)).
- **Builds on:** [0075](0075-parameters-change-through-stations-setup-locked.md) (parameters change through stations), [stations](../design/stations.md), [0069](0069-jobs-institutions-and-daily-lives-one-model-depth-bought-by-attention.md) rule 3 (institutions are queues with staff) and rule 4 (staffing and money come from above), [0070](0070-broken-commitments-are-noticed.md), [spec 39](../tier0/specs/39-orders-and-belief.md) (an edict acts only through the ordinary commands below it; beliefs per pop), [spec 54](../tier0/specs/54-schedules.md) (commitments on the day), the macro layer's "events are detected, never injected", [sandbox parameters](../design/sandbox-parameters.md) §1.2 (collapse pressure is a derived number).

## Decision

1. **No switch.** There is no collapse parameter, event or state that anything sets: not the sandbox panel, not a station, not a scenario. "Collapsed" as a government type ([macro layer](../design/macro-layer.md) §6.3) is a starting shape of a country's stations (which ones exist and who holds them), not a mode the simulation enters.
2. **A lever works only through pops who carry it out.** A station's change goes down the chain of commands ([spec 39](../tier0/specs/39-orders-and-belief.md)); each command below is carried out by the pops at the stations and queues beneath it. A levy with no collectors collects nothing; a curfew with no patrols is a sentence; a hospital with no staff admits nobody. The parameter still reads as set; its effect is what the chain delivered, and the difference is visible.
3. **Pops leave their stations by their own decisions.** A holder stops holding when the pop dies, is absent ([0070](0070-broken-commitments-are-noticed.md)), flees, is not paid, or decides the job is not worth the risk, each by the pop AI from its own parameters: needs, beliefs about the outbreak ([spec 39](../tier0/specs/39-orders-and-belief.md): what it has heard, from whom, how old), ties to its household, money ([spec 32](../tier0/specs/32-stocks-goods-and-money.md)), its day ([spec 54](../tier0/specs/54-schedules.md)). An empty station changes nothing until someone takes it, and who may take it is the scope above's lever ([stations](../design/stations.md) rule 6).
4. **Collapse is the sum of those decisions.** Society fails when enough stations empty, enough queues overflow, enough commitments break and enough chains stop delivering, in that place, at that time. It is read, never written: the share of stations held, the queue lengths, the compliance of each chain (commands delivered over commands issued), the trust that falls when expectations break ([0070](0070-broken-commitments-are-noticed.md)). These are **derived** parameters of the catalogue, the panels of [0051](0051-money-and-resources-shown-follow-the-selection.md) show them for the selection, and the chronicle tells them as what happened to whom.
5. **The loop is closed both ways.** The world acts on pops (the outbreak, the weather, the grid, the shortages); pops act by their parameters (stay, work, hoard, flee, sign); their acts change the numbers other pops read (the station's levers, the queues, the stocks, the beliefs that retelling spreads); and those numbers act on pops again. Nothing in the chain is scripted, and every link traces to its cause ([spec 39](../tier0/specs/39-orders-and-belief.md) root cause). A sandbox option that reaches a station must respect this: it sets a number a pop reads, never what a pop does.
6. **Recovery is the same loop run the other way.** Stations are retaken, queues drain, chains deliver again, trust rebuilds slowly, by the same decisions. No rule declares a place recovered.

## Consequences

- [Stations](../design/stations.md) gains rule 8 (a lever delivers only through the pops below it; collapse is derived) and, per row, the honest question "who carries this out, and what makes them stop".
- [Sandbox parameters](../design/sandbox-parameters.md) §1.6 names the derived readouts of rule 4 as the collapse row of the catalogue; the no-outbreak game (§2) keeps the same loop with other pressures (unemployment, prices, crime).
- The pop AI ([spec 24](../tier0/specs/24-pop-ai.md)) gains, when the stations exist, the decision to hold or leave a station from the pop's own parameters; the first lever that reads it is the police queue's staffing (IQ), where officers who stop coming are the first sign a town is failing.
- A sandbox option that would "switch off" a society (a scenario that starts collapsed) is a starting shape of stations and holders, filled by the generator, never a flag.
