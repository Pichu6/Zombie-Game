# 0015 · Tone: realistic, not gore; one damage system for bodies and buildings

- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Status:** Decided by the user (intent). The shared damage model in [body trauma](../design/body-trauma.md#one-damage-system) is a Proposal.
- **Answers:** the last open question (tone and graphic violence) from [0011](0011-setting-scale-lenses.md) and [0014](0014-open-questions-answered.md).

## Decisions

- **Not gore, but realistic.** Damage and decay look as they would in real life, without exaggeration for shock.
- **Rot is realistic,** layer by layer, as already decided for decay.
- **The body is layered** (skin, fat, muscle, organs, skeleton), so damage can show or represent what is underneath: an exposed bone fracture, a deep cut, an amputation, a body cut in half.
- **Bodies can be broken by the world** the way a wall can break, for example crushed by a collapsing wall.
- **One system could be shared** between body damage and building destruction.

## Why

The game's theme is how real survival is; the damage should teach what actually happened to a body, not entertain with spectacle.
