# 0020 · Activities are automatic by default, simple when played, and items carry their owners' lives

- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** Decided by the user.
- **Answers:** the three questions in [activities](../design/activities.md) after [0019](0019-activities-and-delegation.md).

## Decisions

1. **The player's hands never beat the character's skill.**
2. **Automatic by default.** The first time an activity comes up it is played hands-on, as a tutorial; after that hands-on is optional, like leaving a character on a timed task in Project Zomboid, or the lockpicking and computer mini-game mods. Activities are tasks for pops, an option for the player, and a source of immersion.
3. **Simple, easy and varied,** in the spirit of GTA and Shenmue activities and the War Dogs building system (press a button to build faster). In the Individual layer the play is Project Zomboid-style survival; the possessed pop's tasks become optional objectives (a mix of Project Zomboid, Thunder Tier One and The Sims).
4. **Items carry their owners' lives:** a zombie's belongings come from the person they were; a house key in a zombie's pocket opens that person's real house. From the world map you can pick a city or zone, then a shop, then a pop working there, and see their tasks as objectives.

## Why

The user wants every pop to have a believable life ("lore") that the player can step into, with plenty to do and nothing that feels like a chore.
