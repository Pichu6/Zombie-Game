# 0031 · Restoring lost capabilities, real durations, playable age, time controls

- **Date:** 2026-10-06
- **Status:** Decided by the user (intent below). Models are Proposals in [sandbox parameters](../design/sandbox-parameters.md) and [pop continuity](../design/pop-continuity.md).
- **Answers:** the open point of [0030](0030-pop-continuity-and-capability-loss.md).

## Decisions

1. **Restoring a lost capability is not impossible, but nearly.** The AI never does it on its own. It becomes a human goal or late-game play, and needs trade, cooperation and the like.
2. **Real time is the standard of the simulation:** everything takes a lot of time, as in real life. Action and process durations are real-life durations, with no shortcuts for convenience.
3. **Playable pops have a minimum age;** the player never plays a baby. Younger pops exist, age and can be followed as records, and become playable at that age.
4. **Time can be accelerated, decelerated and paused.**

## Not changed

The default speed in [vision](../vision.md) (1 real hour per game day at 1×; bodies at real walking speed, [0021](0021-recommended-defaults.md)) stays as decided. Whether the user wants a 1:1 "real time" speed as the default instead is asked in [sandbox parameters](../design/sandbox-parameters.md) §15.
