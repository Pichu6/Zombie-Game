# Pichu's original vision (2026-10-05, verbatim, lightly cleaned)

My original idea was based on Project Zomboid, touching the things their game lacks, or any other game I know. But they don't do it for a reason, hahaha.

- In RTS I can command "troops" and give them orders, and then, like in Dungeon Keeper, I can "select" a unit and play it in a localized or immersive way: isometric like Zomboid, Diablo, Thunder Tier One, or 3rd person like The Last of Us, Ghost Recon, GTA.
- Every NPC would be a pop, like SimCity or The Sims style.
- The human body should be better reflected in the simulation: fat, slim, muscled, fit, different traits, etc. From that human comes the zombie, because it is an infected human body. A good human body could also drive animation or movement later on, because the limits are the ones of the real body.
- The world simulation should handle many things games fake: the different grids, fluids, physics, etc.
- In Project Zomboid the map is fixed and all buildings are accessible.
- I like Haven & Hearth systems where everything takes a good amount of time.

We should flesh out all these ideas so we can see what we can actually achieve. Use a couple of agents to juggle ideas in a refinement loop.
