# Worldviews after strange experiences, conversion, conspiracy belief and reputation

**Date:** 2026-10-10. **Status:** research note, not a decision. **Feeds:** [cases and living lore](../design/cases-and-living-lore.md) (§14 strange cases and the outside will), [inhabitants](../design/inhabitants.md), [spec 39](../tier0/specs/39-orders-and-belief.md), [spec 24](../tier0/specs/24-pop-ai.md), research [two-contagions](two-contagions.md) (rules 7–9), [pop-behaviour](pop-behaviour.md) (§1 dispositions). **Evidence:** one research pass on 2026-10-10 (36 web calls). `sourced` rows were read in the paper or page (McNally and Clancy, Bentzen, Sibley and Bulbulia, Douglas et al., the COVID conspiracy meta-analysis, Sommerfeld et al., Jøsang, game wikis); `reported` rows come from abstracts or summaries. Goertzel, Wood et al., Lofland–Stark and Worsley were not read in full. No public survey on belief in the simulation hypothesis was found. **IDs:** WV.

The user's ideas (2026-10-10): after missing time caused by a player's possession, a pop may adopt a whole new explanation of the world (aliens, possession, a religious turn, conspiracy); some believe him and some don't; perhaps a reputation system; and if the player is careless, cults and myths about "someone out there" may form. This note gathers the evidence on how people explain anomalies, convert, believe conspiracies and judge each other, so the game can model it from causes, not scripts.

## What this means for us (Proposal)

All numbers below are `estimate` unless a finding gives them. Nothing is decided. The shape follows the existing rule: nothing supernatural is simulated; a worldview is a belief that changes how other beliefs are received.

### 1. Explanatory frames are data (spec 39, pop-lore §7)

A **frame** is a reusable explanation: `mundane` (sleepwalking, drink, lying), `infection` (always available in this world), `possession`, `divine` (a religious turn), `visitors` (aliens), `hidden_hand` (conspiracy), `place_curse` (folklore). A pop holds frames with a **certainty** 0–1 (the RimWorld number, WV-33, without its conversation spam). Frames are latent in most pops: 73 % of US adults hold at least one paranormal belief (WV-7), so a pop's starting frame weights come from the country's survey shares.

```toml
# data/beliefs/frames.toml
[[frame]]
id = "visitors"
family = "hidden_agent"       # monological family (WV-22, WV-23)
explains = ["missing_time", "marks", "sighting", "sleep_episode"]
needs_carrier = true          # adoptable only if a bonded pop, a place's folklore or media holds it (WV-2)
loading = { absorption = 0.6, reflection = -0.5, anxiety = 0.2 }   # signs from WV-5, WV-9, WV-25; sizes estimate
official_trust = 0.8          # multiplier on Broadcast credibility for holders (WV-20)
compliance = { medical = -0.31, distancing = -0.10, hygiene = 0.0, alt_remedy = 0.42 }  # target correlations, WV-24
[frame.latent_share]          # share of adults holding it latent, by country: survey tables (Gallup WV-7/8, WVS WV-17)
us = 0.24                     # placeholder from the 24–29 % band of WV-8
```

### 2. Trigger → explanation choice (cases §14, spec 24)

When a pop gets an `Unexplained` belief (missing time, a sighting it cannot join, high dissonance), it chooses a frame by a keyed draw over **candidates it can reach**: frames it holds, frames held by its bonds (weighted by bond × their certainty), its place's folklore and broadcast media, plus `mundane` and `infection`. A frame nobody around holds cannot be adopted: the abductees took their story from books, TV and a hypnotherapist (WV-2).

`score(f) = ln(prior_f) + Σ loading_f,k × disposition_k + c × exposure_f + d × fit_f(traces)`

- **prior**: own certainty, else the latent share.
- **dispositions**: add two hidden dispositions to pop-behaviour §1, **absorption** (fantasy proneness, openness to odd experience) and **reflection** (analytic thinking). Abductees scored about 1 SD higher on absorption and far higher on magical ideation (WV-5); reflection improves discernment regardless of side (WV-25). Anxiety and low control raise `hidden_hand` (WV-19).
- **fit**: physical traces choose among frames; marks and waking in odd places are what believers cite (WV-3).
- **Base rate**: sleep paralysis touches at least 15 % of people and almost none adopt abduction (WV-6). So a single episode should almost always end `mundane`; exotic frames need repeats, a high-absorption pop and a carrier nearby. Estimate: ≤ 5 % of first episodes, rising with each repeat.
- **Religious pops intensify rather than convert** (WV-15): a pop with `divine` certainty > 0 gains certainty; a non-holder adopts it rarely.

### 3. How a worldview changes perception and trust (spec 39, TC 7–9)

- **Reception**: an incoming `Told` or `Broadcast` claim that fits a held frame gets confidence × (1 + 0.3 c); one that contradicts it gets × (1 − 0.3 c). `Seen` always wins (TC-7). **No backfire term**: a contradicting sighting or a trusted correction only lowers confidence (WV-26).
- **Monological family**: holding one `hidden_agent` frame raises the prior of the others (WV-22, WV-23).
- **Trust**: `hidden_hand` holders multiply Broadcast credibility (TC rule 9) by `official_trust`, for all sources, not just the one the theory names (WV-20). A cover-up found out (TC rule 9) feeds the frame.
- **Behaviour is a small, targeted effect**, not a switch: the run must reproduce WV-24's ordering. Strong against official medical acts (bite checks, quarantine), weak against distancing, none on personal hygiene, and an increase in "remedies". These are **test targets measured from the run**, never set as constants (the EF rule).

### 4. How others react to his claims (spec 39, cases §14)

Telling "I lost time" or "I saw Pedro at the tower" is a `Tell` like any other (spec 39 rule 6), and it also writes evidence about the **teller**:
- A listener whose frames make the claim plausible gains exposure to the frame (the recruitment path).
- A listener who finds it implausible adds negative evidence on the teller's `credible` and `sane` dimensions (§2.5).
- **Labels raise distance**: 60 % expect violence from someone labelled with schizophrenia, and 45 % would refuse them as a neighbour (WV-32). In this world the sharpest label is `infected` (cases §14): avoidance rises with the listener's `sick` estimate of him.
- **Hearsay can outweigh what you saw**: told evidence moved behaviour even against direct observation (WV-28). It also arrives softened: gossip pulls ratings toward the middle by about 20 % (WV-29).

### 5. Reputation is per-observer beliefs that spread by gossip (spec 39, spec 24)

- **Store**: for observer o, target t and dimension d, the counts `(r, s)` (WV-31). Rating `E = (r+1)/(r+s+2)`; uncertainty `2/(r+s+2)` says "I barely know him."
- **Dimensions** (estimate): `kind` (the image score of WV-30), `honest` (his claims checked out), `sane` (odd claims, missing time), `sick` (infection suspicion), `able` (skill), plus the frames he is known to hold.
- **Evidence**: a `Seen` act adds 1. A `Told` one adds `E_o(teller, honest) × 0.7^hops`, which is spec 39's hop rule used as Jøsang discounting, then shrunk 20 % toward neutral (WV-29).
- **Decay**: `r, s ← λ r, λ s` per day (WV-31). This is TC rule 3's forgetting applied to people; no measured λ was found, so use a placeholder of a 90-day half-life.
- **Traffic**: about two-thirds or more of talk is about people (WV-27), so most `Tell`s carry reputation rather than threat news.
- **Coarse**: at City and Macro, keep summed `(r, s)` per community for notable targets and for groups (household, faction, frame adherents). These are DF's four levels (WV-37), and adding counts is exact under WV-31. A check: reputation only sustains help where the share who know a target's image exceeds cost/benefit (WV-30).

### 6. How groups and movements form (spec 24, spec 39, cases §14 open item)

- **Recruitment is a complex contagion over bonds** (TC rule 4's two-source rule). Adoption needs exposure from at least 2 bonded holders plus time together; weak outside ties speed it; a shock is the turning point (WV-10, WV-11). The missing-time victim's own household is the first audience.
- **Shocks**: the outbreak is an unpredictable disaster, so `divine` certainty rises in holders, while conversions are rarer and fade sooner. Targets: an effect lasting about 9–12 years against 3 for conversions; larger where shocks are rare and income and education are low; none from forecast events (WV-14–WV-16). Christchurch gives the magnitude: +3.4 % net against a −0.9 %/year trend (WV-12).
- **Folklore**: k strange cases in one place with a shared frame write a belief about the place ("the town where people lose time"). This uses spec 39's planned beliefs about places.
- **Coarse form**: per district, the share in each frame as latent, holder or committed, with flows from shocks × candidates, from recruitment, and from decay. It is the TC u/h/a pattern, measured from entity runs.

### 7. An "outside will" from accumulated strange cases (cases §14, spec 39, spec 24)

The `outside_will` frame ("someone out there acts through us") belongs to the `hidden_agent` family. It starts with **no latent share and no carrier**: no public survey measures belief in a simulator (WV-46), and the cargo movements arose from local events, not from an existing doctrine (WV-42, WV-44). A pop can reach it only by **connecting the dots**:
- **When.** It holds at least 3 `Unexplained` beliefs (estimate), its own or told, that share features (missing time, out-of-character acts, sightings that cannot be joined).
- **Who.** Its **pattern perception** passes a threshold. Pattern perception rises with absorption and magical ideation and falls with reflection (WV-5, WV-25, WV-38, r ≈ 0.3).

Fear and uncertainty push ambiguous events toward an agent reading (WV-40). The feedback runs both ways (WV-39, WV-41): once held, the frame raises the pop's pattern perception, so it files more ordinary noise as cases. Only reflection, `mundane` explanations and contradicting `Seen` beliefs bound this.

**What decides whether it arises in a place:**
1. **Case rate and clustering.** The rate is strange cases per 1,000 pops per month, which is the player's carelessness (dissonance × episodes). The cases must also sit in one place and travel along bonds, so the dots meet in the same heads.
2. **Competitors.** `infection` explains missing time first in this world, so a place where the infection frame is strong absorbs the cases. `divine` absorbs them where religiosity is high.
3. **Anxiety and loss of control** raise hidden-agent frames (WV-19); the outbreak supplies both.
4. **A prophet.** This is one early holder with high certainty, sociability and a good `honest` rating (Worsley's prophet, WV-43). Recruitment then follows §2.6's two-bond rule.

**What decides whether it lasts.** Repression did not stop John Frum (WV-44). The game equivalents of what carried it:
- a recurring shared act (the Tanna Army parade, the intensive interaction of WV-10);
- an identity tie (the claimed bond with the US, WV-45);
- continued evidence: a careless player keeps feeding it.

Without these it fades like Vailala, strong for about 3 years and gone in under 10 (WV-42), the same span as Bentzen's conversion effect (WV-15).

**Reactions and the coarse form.** Non-holders rate believers lower on `sane` (WV-32). At City and Macro, the case rate and the holder share per district are stocks. The take-off threshold is measured from entity runs, never set.

The twist: these pops are right. The game never confirms it, and a careful player leaves nothing to connect.

### 8. Tests (A3 style, many seeds)

- Pops who adopt an exotic frame after missing time have higher absorption than those who do not (WV-5 direction).
- The correlations between frame and compliance come out in WV-24's order.
- No pop's confidence ever rises after a contradicting `Seen`.
- After a shock, religiosity rises within holders more than conversions do, and decays (WV-15).
- Reputation summed per community equals the sum of per-observer counts.
- With careful possession (low dissonance) no `outside_will` holder appears in any seed. Above a measured case rate, holders appear in clusters along bonds, and they fade within about 10 game years once the cases stop (§2.7).
